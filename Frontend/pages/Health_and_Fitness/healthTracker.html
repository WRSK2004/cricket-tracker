Health Metrics Calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI)

Formula: BMI = weight (kg) / (height (m))^2

Categories:

  • Underweight: BMI < 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 ≤ BMI < 25
  • Overweight: 25 ≤ BMI < 30
  • Obesity: BMI ≥ 30

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor Equation):

  • For men: BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) - 5 × age (years) + 5
  • For women: BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) - 5 × age (years) - 161

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

Definition: TDEE represents the total number of calories you burn in a day, taking into account your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and your activity level.

Formula: TDEE = BMR × Activity Level

Activity Levels:

  • Sedentary (little or no exercise): TDEE = BMR × 1.2
  • Lightly active (light exercise/sports 1-3 days/week): TDEE = BMR × 1.375
  • Moderately active (moderate exercise/sports 3-5 days/week): TDEE = BMR × 1.55
  • Very active (hard exercise/sports 6-7 days a week): TDEE = BMR × 1.725
  • Extra active (very hard exercise/sports & physical job or 2x training): TDEE = BMR × 1.9

Macronutrient Distribution

General Recommendations:

  • Carbohydrates: 45-65% of total daily calories
  • Proteins: 10-35% of total daily calories
  • Fats: 20-35% of total daily calories
