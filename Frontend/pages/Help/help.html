Getting Started

Welcome to Cricket Tracker! This guide will help you get started with using our app to track your cricket practice sessions, health, and fitness.

To use the 'Practice Sessions' feature, navigate to the Practice Sessions tab from the top navigation bar. Here you can start a new practice session, view your past sessions, and analyze your performance with video playback and feedback.

To use the 'Health & Fitness' feature, go to the Health & Fitness tab. Here you can log your workouts, track your fitness goals, and monitor your progress over time with various health metrics.

To customize your experience, visit the Settings tab. Here you can update your profile information, adjust notification preferences, and manage your account settings.

FAQs

Cricket Tracker is an app designed to help cricket players track their practice sessions, monitor their health and fitness, and improve their performance with AI-driven insights.

Navigate to Practice Sessions, click 'Create New Session', select a drill type, follow the tutorial, then upload your video for AI analysis.

We currently offer a variety of drills including batting, bowling, and fielding exercises. New drills are added regularly based on user feedback and demand.

Batting Stance: The position a batsman adopts before facing a delivery — feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent, bat resting near the crease.

Front Foot Defense: A defensive shot played by stepping forward towards the ball to block it safely.

Back Foot Defense: A defensive shot played by stepping back towards the stumps to block a short-pitched delivery.

Crease: The white lines marked on the pitch that define the batsman's safe zone.

Wicket: The three stumps and two bails that the bowler aims to hit to dismiss the batsman.

Over: A set of six deliveries bowled by the same bowler.

LBW (Leg Before Wicket): A dismissal where the ball hits the batsman's leg and would have gone on to hit the stumps.

No Ball: An illegal delivery, usually when the bowler oversteps the crease.

Wide: A delivery bowled too far from the batsman to be playable, resulting in an extra run.

Fielding Positions: Named positions around the pitch where fielders stand, such as slip, gully, mid-on, and mid-off.

After uploading your video for AI analysis, you will receive detailed feedback on your performance. This feedback includes metrics such as accuracy, speed, and technique. Use this information to identify areas for improvement and track your progress over time.

Your BMI (Body Mass Index), BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate), and TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) are calculated based on the personal information you provide, such as age, gender, weight, height, and activity level. These metrics help you understand your body composition and energy needs.

To update your profile settings, navigate to the Settings tab, update your batting hand, bowling hand, bowling style, and role using the dropdown menus, then click 'Save Profile Settings'.

Video playback issues can be caused by several factors including unsupported video formats, browser compatibility, or network issues. Please ensure your video is in a supported format (e.g., MP4), try using a different browser, and check your internet connection.
